# Decomposting Theory

Decomposting belongs to the family of **theories of development**, alongside approaches such as rapid development. **Decomposting === recovery and recomposition.** The two names identify the same theory, recursive method, and ethic of proper construction. The approach is agnostic about the kind of material being developed. It applies to material you own or material you are permitted to use for the intended purpose. The applicable rules and authority depend on the material and the proposed action.

## Theory, recursive method, and ethic

**As a theory of construction**, decomposting holds that decomposition, composting, and composition happen together. What you learn about a source's parts and relationships changes what you can carry forward. The candidate taking shape changes what you need to examine in the source. Composting is the work of deciding what material can feed new growth as the candidate is composed.

**As a method**, decomposting is recursive. It was itself created recursively, and recursion is its strongest application with transmigration. When a candidate exposes a dependency that needs closer examination, apply the same method to that dependency within its authorized boundary. Bring the result back into the parent candidate and check the whole construction again. End a pass when its contribution is adequately represented for the intended use. Carry forward an unresolved condition when a required dependency cannot be settled within scope.

**As an ethic**, decomposting governs what may be carried forward, how the material may be used, and what can be claimed about the result. Preserve provenance, respect the purpose and limits of permission, and assess the resulting construction before accepting or ejecting it as a seed.

## The quadrangle of proper construction

| Corner | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Legal | Do applicable law and binding obligations permit this use, transformation, or transfer? |
| Allowed | Has the relevant authority permitted this specific action for this purpose and scope? |
| Letter | Does the construction follow the written terms and requirements that govern it? |
| Spirit | Does the construction honor the purpose those terms and requirements serve? |

**Legal and allowed** form the moral decision plane. **Letter and spirit** test how the construction is carried out. Each corner must be established on its own. A failed or unresolved corner stops the affected step. Record what remains unresolved and which decision or evidence is needed. Apply the quadrangle at each recursive pass and again before accepting or ejecting the resulting seed.

## Example with a repository lookup seed

A lookup seed carries a response contract and a disclosure boundary. During extraction, the candidate exposes a dependency on how the receiving system checks evidence locators. A recursive pass examines that dependency, retains the relevant disclosure rule by reference, and states which local decision the receiving system must supply. The result then returns to the parent seed for review.

Suppose a candidate omits withheld text but includes a locator that would reveal the same material. That candidate can satisfy a narrow reading of the written requirement while defeating its purpose. Proper construction checks both the letter and the spirit and resolves the disclosure failure before the seed can be accepted. The legal and allowed questions still apply to the proposed use and transfer of each retained record.
